# Avalancha

An incremental game played on a real sandpile: grains fall, cells topple, and every topple pays out Energy.

The pile on screen is the actual abelian sandpile simulation, not decoration. The avalanches you see are the ones paying you, and their sizes follow the model's power law: most are tiny, a few are enormous.

Version 1.0 covers two layers: **Sand** (about 3–4 hours, to the first Infinity) and **Crystallization** (about 15 more hours: Crystals, the Lattice, relics, Breaking Infinity, Strata, eight Defects and the Frost). Full automation, 12 notations, achievements with cosmetic rewards, a "How it works" screen with the exact formulas, procedurally generated music, offline progress up to 2 h at ×0.1, save export/import.

No ads, no real-money purchases.
