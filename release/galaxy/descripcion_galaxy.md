# galaxy.click — Avalancha

## Título

Avalancha

## Descripción corta (una línea)

An incremental game played on a real sandpile: grains fall, cells topple, and every topple pays out Energy.

## Descripción

The pile on screen isn't decoration — it's a real abelian sandpile simulation. Grains drop onto the center of a grid; any cell holding four grains topples and passes one grain to each neighbor, setting off chain reactions. Every topple pays you, so the avalanches you watch are literally your income, and their sizes follow the model's power law: most are tiny, a few are enormous.

**Version 1.0 has two layers:**

- **Layer 0 — Sand** (about 3–4 hours): grains per second, colors that make each height of sand worth more, Compression (prestige), all the way to the first Infinity.
- **Layer 1 — Crystallization** (about 15 more hours): Crystals, the Lattice (a skill tree that keeps opening up), relics to socket and fuse, Breaking Infinity, the Strata funnel, eight Defects (challenges that change the rules of the pile) and the Frost.

**Also:**

- Every Infinity leaves you a mandala of your pile, saveable as a 2048×2048 image.
- A "How it works" screen with the exact formula behind each system, using your own numbers.
- Full automation, 12 number notations, achievements with cosmetic palettes, frames and effects.
- Procedurally generated sound and music.
- Offline progress as a small nudge (up to 2 h at ×0.1), save export/import, 3 automatic backups.
- Desktop and mobile, colorblind palette, full keyboard controls, battery saver. English and Spanish (Settings).
