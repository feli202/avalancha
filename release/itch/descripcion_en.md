# Avalancha

**An incremental game played on a real sandpile.** Grains fall, cells topple, and every topple pays out Energy.

Avalancha is built on the abelian sandpile, a classic model from physics. Grains drop onto the center of a grid; any cell holding four grains topples and passes one grain to each neighbor, which can set off a chain reaction. The pile on screen is the real simulation, not decoration: the avalanches you see are the ones paying you, and their sizes follow the power law the model is famous for. Most are tiny. A few are enormous.

## What's in version 1.0

- **Layer 0, Sand** (about 3–4 hours): buy grains per second and colors that make each height of sand worth more, then Compress (prestige) your way to the first Infinity.
- **Layer 1, Crystallization** (about 15 more hours): Crystals, the Lattice (a skill tree that keeps opening), relics to socket and fuse, Breaking Infinity, the Strata funnel, eight Defects (challenges that change the rules of the pile) and the Frost.
- **The pile is the reward.** Upgrades change how it looks, and every Infinity leaves you a mandala you can save as a 2048×2048 image.
- **"How it works"** shows the exact formula behind each system with your own numbers plugged in.
- Full automation, 12 number notations (including a few odd ones), achievements with cosmetic palettes, frames and effects, procedurally generated sound and music.
- Offline progress as a small nudge (up to 2 h at ×0.1), save export and import, 3 automatic backups.
- Plays on desktop and phone, installable as an app, colorblind palette, full keyboard controls, battery saver. English and Spanish.
