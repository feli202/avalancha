# Publication copy (galaxy.click, itch.io)

## Title

Avalancha

## Short description

An incremental game played on a real sandpile: grains fall, cells topple, and every topple pays out energy.

## Long description

Avalancha is an incremental game built on the abelian sandpile, a well-known model from physics. Grains fall onto the center of a grid; any cell holding four grains topples and passes one grain to each neighbor, which can set off a chain reaction. Every topple gives you Energy, which you spend on more grains per second, on colors that make each height of sand worth more, and later on Compression, a prestige reset that raises the threshold for what comes next. This demo is Layer 0: from your first grain to the first Infinity, about three hours if you play attentively, longer if you leave it running in the background.

What sets it apart is that the pile on screen is the real simulation, not decoration. The avalanches you see are the ones paying you, and their sizes follow the power law the model is known for, so most are tiny and a few are enormous. Upgrades change what the pile looks like, and the "How it works" screen shows the exact formula behind each system with your own numbers plugged in. The game has offline progress, save export and import, 24 achievements that unlock cosmetic palettes, effects and frames, procedurally generated sound and music, a colorblind palette, full keyboard controls, and English and Spanish text.

The next layer, Crystallization, is in development and starts where this demo ends: reaching Infinity leaves your save ready for it. Layer 0 is deliberately simple in its choices; the next layer is where the bigger decisions are meant to come in.
